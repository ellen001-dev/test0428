## 3. Why Square Is a Retail Giant But a Contractor's Nightmare

Here's the fundamental problem: **Square was built for coffee shops, retail stores, and restaurants**. It assumes your customers will walk up to a counter, hand you a card, and leave.

Field service doesn't work that way. Your workflow is completely different:
1. Customer calls with a problem
2. You schedule a technician visit
3. Tech drives to the location (often 30+ minutes)
4. Diagnoses the issue on-site
5. Presents multiple repair/replacement options
6. Takes photos for documentation
7. Customer approves and signs
8. Payment is collected
9. Follow-up and review requests

Square handles exactly one step of that process: payment collection. Everything else? You're on your own.

### The Critical Features Square Lacks

#### 1. No Multi-Option Estimates
Square can only send a single invoice. You can't present customers with:
- Basic repair: $1,200
- Premium repair: $1,800  
- Full replacement: $3,500

This limitation alone costs contractors thousands in lost upsell opportunities. Without options, customers default to the cheapest choice.

#### 2. No GPS Dispatch & Route Optimization
With Square, you have no idea where your technicians are. You're stuck:
- Calling techs constantly: "Where are you?"
- Manually planning routes: "Who's closest to this emergency call?"
- Wasting fuel and time with inefficient scheduling

Housecall Pro's GPS tracking alone saves contractors an average of **2.3 hours per technician per week**.

#### 3. No Membership/Recurring Revenue Management
This is the biggest profit killer. Square cannot:
- Automatically track maintenance contract renewals
- Schedule recurring service visits
- Process recurring payments
- Send renewal reminders

Contractors without membership programs see **3x lower customer retention** and **40% less annual revenue** than those with them.
