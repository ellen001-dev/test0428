## 3. Pipeline Visibility: Kanban Board vs. ServiceTitan Dispatch

Roofing projects are long-cycle. You need visibility into every stage:

### JOBNIMBUS: KANBAN BOARD PERFECTION
JobNimbus's Kanban board is a game-changer for roofing:

- **Visual Pipeline**: See all jobs in progress at a glance
- **Custom Stages**: Create stages like "Lead → Measurement → Proposal → Contract → Scheduled → In Progress → Completed"
- **Drag-and-Drop**: Move jobs between stages with a click
- **Team Collaboration**: Assign tasks and track progress

For managing a pipeline of 50+ roofing projects, JobNimbus's Kanban board is unmatched.

### SERVICETITAN: CALENDAR-FIRST DISPATCH
ServiceTitan is designed for **short-cycle residential services**:

- **Calendar View**: Great for daily scheduling
- **Dispatch Board**: Perfect for assigning same-day jobs

If you're doing mostly small repairs and maintenance, ServiceTitan's dispatch shines. But for roofing projects that take weeks to complete, it feels clunky.
